Projects

FACEit

2026 · Turn-based combat

I developed FACEit solo in two days for Global Game Jam 2026 and used AI to generate the assets. It's a turn-based combat game where each opponent is a mask shaped by an emotion. On your turn, you can attack, defend, observe, or talk. Figuring out how to respond to each mask is part of the fight.

Indian SuperMart

2026 · 3D retail simulation

I built Indian SuperMart from scratch to recreate some of the mechanics from Supermarket Simulator on Steam. You stock shelves, set prices, and serve customers at the till. I made every asset myself, learning to model in Blender and texture in Substance 3D Painter along the way. I wanted to understand what goes into bringing a 3D object into a game so I could communicate better with the artists, designers, and developers I work with in the future.
